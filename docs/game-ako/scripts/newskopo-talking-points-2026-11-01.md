# NewsKoPo Talking Points — November 1, 2026

**Assumption:** Sunday, November 1, 2026 livestream; approximately 30–40 minutes. English, conversational delivery. Co-host prompts are questions, not scripted answers.

**Source note:** The text of the supplied Game Ako Chronicles articles was not accessible in the web reader, and the indexed site showed different stories. The two game-review segments below are cues for Arohbi's own impressions, not invented review details.

## Opening

- “Welcome to NewsKoPo. Tonight, we’re looking at what’s happening around Steam Next Fest, with two unusual tank games as our examples, then a long-awaited Nintendo service update for the Philippines, a FromSoftware delay, and a new Death Stranding anime teaser.”
- Set up the main conversation: Steam Next Fest is a huge discovery opportunity, but when thousands of demos compete for attention, how do players find games worth their time?
- Invite viewers to share which demos they tried and what caught their eye.

## Marquee topic: Steam Next Fest, demos, and the discovery problem

The October edition ran October 19–26. Valve describes Next Fest as a chance for players to try demos and discover upcoming games, and for developers to gather feedback and build an audience. The next edition is planned for February 2027. [Steamworks: October 2026 Next Fest](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest/2026october?language=english)

- **Talk track:** “A week of demos sounds like a buffet. But the bigger the buffet gets, the harder it is to know where to start.”
- **Reported scale:** PC Gamer found 8,011 matches on the event’s press preview page before it opened. Treat that as a reported listing count, not a confirmed count of distinct playable demos. [PC Gamer report](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/steam-week-in-review-steam-next-fest-has-over-8-000-demos-as-worthless-vibe-coded-slop-levels-up/)
- **Suggested angle:** The useful question may be less “Was this Next Fest good or bad?” and more “What helps a small or unusual game get noticed amid so many choices?”
- **Keep the AI conversation precise:** There was public discussion about low-effort and AI-associated submissions. Separate that broader debate from games that simply feature AI as a story or gameplay element. For example, *SLAM TANK* has you fight an AI’s machine army; that alone doesn’t tell us whether generative AI was used to make the game.
- **Co-host prompts:**
  - “How did you decide which demos to try?”
  - “Did you find something you probably wouldn’t have noticed outside the event?”
  - “Does a large event page make discovery exciting, overwhelming, or both?”
- **Transition:** “Two of the demos I played show how odd and varied a Next Fest lineup can get: one tank swings into combat, and another apparently knows martial arts.”

## Demo example 1: *SLAM TANK*

Your review: [Before Waldas: Slam Tank and Tank Fu demo review](https://game-ako-chronicles.vercel.app/articles/before-waldas-slam-tank-and-tank-fu-demo-review)

- **Verified setup:** Steam describes *SLAM TANK* as a stylish, single-player tank action game where you swing the cannon barrel and ram through an AI machine army. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5186030/SLAM_TANK/)
- **Your talking points:** Share what stood out in your hands-on demo: what felt satisfying, what surprised you, and whether the central tank-action idea held up over the session.
- **Connect it to the marquee topic:** It’s an example of a premise that can grab attention in a crowded demo festival. Did the demo make you want to see more, or did it mainly work as a memorable first impression?
- **Co-host prompt:** “If you saw that description while scrolling Next Fest, would it make you stop and download the demo?”

## Demo example 2: *Tank Fu*

- **Verified setup:** Steam calls *Tank Fu* an experimental, over-the-top character-action fighting game. Its page describes tanks dashing, flying, and punching, with a pre-alpha playtest that includes a move tutorial and an arena challenge. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5106210/Tank_Fu/)
- **Your talking points:** Bring in your own impressions of the controls, combos, presentation, and how much the demo communicated its potential.
- **Connect it to the marquee topic:** *Tank Fu* is a second example of how a short demo can introduce an immediately legible hook. The follow-up question is whether the play experience makes that hook compelling.
- **Co-host prompt:** “Which concept would you be more curious to revisit: a tank smashing through an army, or a tank doing character-action combos?”

**Transition:** “From games making their first impression through a demo, let’s move to a platform update that could change how Filipino Nintendo players buy and play online.”

## Nintendo eShop and Nintendo Switch Online coming to the Philippines

Nintendo says eShop and Nintendo Switch Online services are scheduled to begin in the Philippines on **November 10** for Nintendo Switch and Switch 2. Its announcement says eShop users can buy and download games and DLC, and purchase Nintendo Switch Online membership. Nintendo lists online play, classic-game access, and Save Data Cloud among the membership features. [Nintendo announcement](https://www.nintendo.com/ph/news/article/tngPVu8X1gJcAMRvRJR7M)

- **Talk track:** “This is a concrete step toward official Nintendo digital services in the Philippines. Players will have a local eShop and access to Nintendo Switch Online services.”
- **Keep expectations grounded:** Nintendo’s announcement confirms the launch date and service categories. It does **not** specify local game prices, payment options, the full local catalog, or how existing accounts and purchases will be handled.
- **Co-host prompts:**
  - “What would you want to know before switching your account region?”
  - “Which matters most to you: buying games locally, online play, classic games, or cloud saves?”
- **Viewer prompt:** “What’s the first thing you want to check when the Philippine eShop opens?”
- **Transition:** “Speaking of Switch 2 news, one of its more unusual exclusives is taking longer to arrive.”

## *The Duskbloods* moves to 2027

FromSoftware moved *The Duskbloods* from its 2026 window to 2027. The studio says it needs more time for final adjustments to the game’s rules and balance after the August network test, with the aim of strengthening its combat, strategic options, and player expression. It’s a Switch 2 multiplayer action game for up to eight players. [FromSoftware announcement](https://www.fromsoftware.jp/jp/pressrelease/20261029_theduskbloods_postponement.html)

- **Talk track:** “The key detail is that the delay is now official. FromSoftware hasn’t given a specific 2027 date.”
- **Discussion angle:** For a multiplayer game, rules and balance are central to the experience. More time could help the team refine those systems, but the announcement doesn’t tell us what the final changes will be.
- **Co-host prompts:**
  - “Does this kind of game benefit from more network-test feedback before launch?”
  - “What would you want to learn next: a new test, more gameplay, or a firmer release window?”
- **Transition:** “One more reveal takes us from a game world to a new story set inside it.”

## *Death Stranding Isolations* anime teaser

Kojima Productions shared new details and a teaser on October 30. The animated series is planned for 2027 on Disney+ or other Disney services. It tells a new story separate from the games, set in North America after Sam reconnects the continent, and follows a young man and woman. The studio says it will use hand-drawn 2D animation, with Takayuki Sano directing and Tow Ubukata handling series composition and screenplay. [Kojima Productions announcement](https://www.kojimaproductions.jp/en/node/306)

- **Talk track:** “The interesting promise is a new story in the same world, rather than a direct retelling of the games.”
- **Discussion angle:** *Death Stranding* is built around connection, isolation, and the consequences of reconnecting a fractured world. The new story’s setting—after the Chiral Network has reconnected North America—gives the anime room to explore what happens next.
- **Co-host prompts:**
  - “Would you rather see Sam’s story continue, or follow new characters in the same world?”
  - “What would hand-drawn animation bring to the atmosphere?”
- **Caveat:** The announcement doesn’t identify Philippine streaming availability or give a specific release date.
