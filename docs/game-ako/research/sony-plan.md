# Sony's Plan with their Brand

> Caveat: This is a speculative plan based on the current situation of Sony and the gaming industry. This does not contain other issue that the publisher is facing such as game preservation and digital ownership. Those topics are worth exploring but are not the focus of this plan.

## The Philippines Context

In the Philippines, Sony presence is significant in the electronics and entertainment sectors. Playstation is a very recognized brand among gamers, while Sony TV's and audio products are popular among consumers. The brand has a long-standing reputation for quality and brand recognition. By the release of PSN (PlayStation Network) fans are thinking why Sony does not have a more localized approach to their services and products in the Philippines. Most Filipino gamers create PSN accounts using foreign addresses as a workaround to access content. There is not official Philippine PlayStation Store, so gamers are paying for content in foreign currencies, which can be inconvenient and costly.

There are a lot of factors that contribute to this situation, but two of I think that is most relevant are the following:

1. **Goverment Regulations**: The Philippines has specific regulations about establishing businesses in the country, they call the 60-40 rule, which states that foreign companies can only own 40% of a business in the Philippines. This makes it difficult for foreign companies to establish a local presence and offer localized services.
2. **Sony's Business Operations**: Sony presence here is tied up to the hardware and appliances where their brand is well established and PSN is just part of the Playstation brand as a whole which is a hardware brand. Sony need to have localized services and support to make PSN accessible to the Filipino market.

## Playstation's Current Situation

Sony is currently experiencing difficulties in their business operation globally. Below are some of the issues and challenges that Sony is facing:

1. **Balooning Costs**: Sony has been facing increasing costs in their operations due to various factors such as supply chain disruptions, rising production costs, and increased competition. This has put pressure on their profit margins and overall financial performance.
2. **Community Engagement**: Sony has been criticized for anti-consumer practices, such as the removal of disc support for the future PS5 games, which has led to backlash from the gaming community.
3. **Competition**: Sony is facing stiff competition from other gaming companies, such as Microsoft and Nintendo, which have been gaining market share in the gaming industry. This has put pressure on Sony to innovate and differentiate their products and services to stay competitive. Also the fallback of PS5 games releasing on PC has been a big blow to the PS5 console sales for both hardware and software.
4. **Game Quality**: Recent releases of 1st party games feels like a rehash of previous titles and keeping games risk averse. This can be led to decline in creativity among 1st party studios and a lack of innovation in game design.
5. **Lack of Localized Services**: As mentioned earlier, Sony has not established a localized presence in the Philippines, which has led to inconvenience for Filipino gamers who have to create foreign accounts to access content. This lack of localized services can lead to a loss of potential customers and revenue for Sony.
6. **Brand Perception**: Sony's brand perception has been affected by various factors, including the issues mentioned above. This can lead to a decline in brand loyalty and customer trust, which can impact sales and revenue.
7. **Market Saturation**: The gaming market is becoming increasingly saturated, with many companies vying for consumer attention. This can make it difficult for Sony to stand out and attract new customers, especially in a competitive market like the Philippines.
8. **3rd Party Support**: 3rd party publishers are what keeps the lights on for the platform but now it faces challenges as 3rd party publishers are now releasing their games on PC and other platforms, which can lead to a decline in exclusive content for the PlayStation platform.

## Sony's Strengths

1. **Content Powerhouse**: Sony has a strong portfolio of entertainment content, including movies, music, and games. This gives them a competitive advantage in the entertainment industry and allows them to leverage their content across multiple platforms.
2. **First-Party Studios**: Sony has a strong lineup of first-party game studios, which allows them to develop exclusive content for their gaming platforms. This can help differentiate their products and services from competitors and attract new customers.
3. **Hardware Expertise**: Sony has a long-standing reputation for producing high-quality hardware, including gaming consoles, TVs, and audio equipment. This expertise can help them develop innovative products and services that meet the needs of consumers.

## Sony's Grand Plan

Will all of this information above, I think Sony will pivot into something that could really shape the future of the company as a whole. There would be a consolidation of their businesses from the hardware and entertainment sectors into a more unified approach that focuses on content and services. Below are some of the potential strategies that Sony could implement:

1. **The Netflix Approach**: Sony could pivot into a subscription-based model for their content, similar to Netflix. They can leverage the already established PlayStation Network as the entry point for all of their content, including movies, music, and games. They can tailor fit the tier of subscription depending on the content that the user wants to access. This approach can solve the issue of localized services in the Philippines and other countries where PlayStation Network is not supported. By offering a subscription-based model, Sony can provide access to their content without the need for localized services, which can help them reach a wider audience and increase revenue.
2. **PS6 as the Entry Hardware**: Sony could position the next PlayStation console (PS6) as the entry point for their content ecosystem. By offering exclusive content and features on the PS6, they can incentivize users to purchase the console and access their content. This approach can help drive hardware sales and increase revenue for Sony.
3. **Sony's Plan of Content Synergy**: This could potentially capitalize on Sony's approach of Content Synergy as stated from their previous business stratrgy plan. By leveraging their content portfolio across multiple platforms, Sony can create a more unified and seamless experience for users. This can help differentiate their products and services from competitors and attract new customers.

## Summary

- PSN will be decoupled from PlayStation hardware and will be a standalone service that will be available on multiple platforms, including mobile devices, smart TVs, and PCs but games will only be available on PlayStation consoles.
- Sony will leverage their content portfolio to offer a subscription-based model for their content, similar to Netflix. This will allow them to reach a wider audience and increase revenue.
- Content Synergy will be a key strategy for Sony, allowing them to leverage their content across multiple platforms and create a more unified and seamless experience for users.
