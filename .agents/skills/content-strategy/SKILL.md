# Content Strategy

Turn the supplied goal or topic into a small plan Arohbi can realistically execute. Read `context/game-ako.md` for platform roles, audience, and the current six-week pilot cadence, and `context/game-ako-series.md` for series roles and formats. If Arohbi provides newer channel information, use it instead.

## Planning rules

- YouTube is the home base. Long-form podcasts and commentary belong on YouTube and should consider search and suggested discovery.
- Facebook primarily promotes YouTube through Reels, announcements, and community posts, each with a clear YouTube follow-through. Preserve NewsKoPo's current Facebook livestream presence and YouTube simulcast during the six-week pilot; review the transition after the pilot.
- Blog articles can extend a topic in writing; plan them only when they fit the goal.
- Treat audience figures as dated context, not live analytics; don't infer engagement or growth without current data.

## Workflow

1. Identify the goal, audience, topic, requested formats, available time, and any deadline from the brief. Ask one clarifying question only when a missing detail would change the plan materially; otherwise state a reasonable assumption.
2. Choose a small number of achievable pieces. For each, state topic/angle, intended audience, format, platform, timing, and the reason it belongs in the plan.
3. Sequence short-form clips or companion posts after an anchor livestream, podcast, or commentary video when source material is available. Mark dependencies and time-sensitive items.
4. Use the six-week cadence in `context/game-ako.md` as a starting pilot, not a quota. Prefer a consistent cadence the creator can sustain over a large burst of work. Don't invent analytics or claim a schedule is optimal without evidence.
5. Treat the reported change from about 190 to about 195 YouTube subscribers as dated context, not a target or a measured growth rate. Do not infer performance without supplied analytics.
6. Return a concise Markdown plan. Include next steps or a handoff to the relevant scriptwriter or social-post writer when useful.

## Boundaries

This skill plans content; it does not draft scripts, titles, descriptions, or standalone social copy. Route those tasks to the corresponding writing skill (`long-form-scriptwriting`, `short-form-scriptwriting`, `youtube-packaging`, `social-post-writing`, or `article-writing`). Do not publish. Save a plan only when Arohbi requests it, following `context/artifacts.md` and saving under `docs/game-ako/plans/`.
