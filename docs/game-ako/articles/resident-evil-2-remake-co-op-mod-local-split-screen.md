The 2019 remake of *Resident Evil 2* just became a two-player survival horror — on one PC, anyway. A fan-made mod called **"Local Couch Co-Op"** (formerly "HUNK'y Friend") adds local co-op to *Resident Evil 2 Remake*, per GameSpew and DSOGaming. It launched on Nexus Mods on October 4 and was recently updated to v1.4, which added vertical split-screen. The mod is free and requires the Steam PC version only.

**How the co-op mod works**

Player 2 can join as HUNK or one of around a dozen other characters — including Ada, Sherry, and Tofu — with separate inventories, item sharing, and a revive mechanic. You can play with two controllers, or keyboard and mouse plus one controller. Installing it needs REFramework, by placing `dinput8.dll` alongside `re2.exe`; the mod does not modify `re2.exe` or the engine itself.

**Who made it — and the catch**

Modder Shopcreeper01 explains his motivation simply: he built it so his wife could play a game she was too scared to play alone. That heartwarming pitch comes with honest caveats: it's fan-made and can be buggy, and one community tip is to turn off ray tracing to hold 60 fps — a single-user report, not a tested benchmark. It's also PC-only, so console players are out of luck. Philippine outlet GamingPH covered the release, so local players hunting for a couch co-op scare session know it's real.

For now, local co-op is the only way to play *RE2 Remake* with a friend. An online, two-instance co-op variant is reportedly in development, but it has no public release yet — don't hunt for download links just yet.
