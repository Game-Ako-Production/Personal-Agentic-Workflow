# Updated Review Process

## 1. Recommended Scope

I kept your three main scopes. Gameplay now has its own scope, and I added the items that were missing. Items marked **(new)** were not in your first list.

**Technical**

- Visuals
- Art Direction
- Audio (new): music, sound effects, voice acting
- Performance: frame rate, loading times, crashes, bugs
- Controls and Camera (new): how the game feels in your hands, controller support
- Settings and Accessibility (new): graphics options, subtitles, remapping

**Gameplay (new scope)**

- Core Gameplay: the main thing you do again and again
- Combat or Main Mechanics: depends on the game type
- Progression: upgrades, skills, rewards
- Difficulty and Balance (new)
- Pacing: does the game stay fun or does it get boring

**Content**

- Game Direction: quest design and level design
- Content Amount and Replay Value (new): how long it is and how much there is to do
- Quality of Life Features: map, fast travel, save system, UI
- Online or Co-op (new, only if the game has it)
- Monetization (new): microtransactions, DLC, season passes

**Story**

- Narrative
- Characters
- World Building
- Localization (new): language options, quality of subtitles or translation

**Why these additions?** Many Filipino gamers use mid-range PCs and controllers, so performance, controls, and settings matter a lot. Monetization also matters when people are spending hard-earned money. You do not need to cover every item in every review. Mention the ones that stood out, good or bad.

## 2. Updated Process

**Rules**

- Before Waldas is the Game Ako take on reviewing games.
- The purpose is to give informative opinions on games.
- The baseline is 5-10 hours of gameplay. This can change depending on the scale of the game.
- You do not need to finish the game. The review is a first impression only.

**Test Setup**

- Always show the platform and hardware used (PC specs or console).
- Show the settings used, such as resolution and graphics preset.
- Show hours played.

**Spoiler Rule**

- No spoilers in Before Waldas. Save story details for GALore.

**Review Format**

- Write pros and cons under each scope: Technical, Gameplay, Content, and Story.
- Keep each paragraph to 8 sentences max.
- End with the Review Label and a short reason.

**Review Label**

- **Day One:** Must buy. Pre-order or buy at full price.
- **Wishlist - Wait for patch:** Wait for a patch, or add it to your backlog.
- **Wishlist - Wait for sale:** Wait for a sale, or add it to your backlog.
- **Skip:** Don't waste your time here. No value to offer.

**Update Rule**

- Revisit the game when a huge update or expansion is released.
