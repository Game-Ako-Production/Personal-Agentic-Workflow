Sony unveiled a new PlayStation 5 color variant today — but it will ship for Japan only. The **"PlayStation 5 Digital Edition Midnight Black (Japanese language only)"** was announced in an October 13 PlayStation.Blog Japan post and launches November 19, 2026, per Gematsu. The disc-less console is priced at ¥55,000 tax included — about US$350 at current exchange rates per Gematsu, with no official Philippine price announced.

**What's in the box**

The full bundle includes the console (825GB SSD, model CFI-2200B02S), one DualSense controller, horizontal feet, cables, printed materials, and a preinstalled *Astro's Playroom* — a complete package rather than a shell. A standalone "Midnight Black" disc drive also launches the same day at a reported ¥11,980.

**The Japan lock**

Like the "Japanese Language Only" PS5 from October 2025 — also ¥55,000 — this variant can only be used inside Japan, pairing a Japanese-language console with a PlayStation account that has Japan set as country or region. No global availability has been announced. It's also separate from the earlier global Midnight Black Collection accessories.

**The Philippine take**

For Filipino gamers, this is more industry-watch material than a shopping tip. Sony keeps slicing out Japan-specific SKUs, but the account and region lock make this model impractical for buyers here, there's no official PHP price to compare against, and importing it would mean living with a locked console and account. If you really want a black PS5, the global Midnight Black Collection accessories remain the more realistic path.
