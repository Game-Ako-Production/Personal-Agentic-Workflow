This will take several episode for the game to fully analyze. The inspiration for this is from `Resonant Arc` but the approach is different. Instead of meticulously analyze the game, we will discuss it in a more Filipino way and approach. Discuss the game story and mechanics, then towards the end we include our interpretation of the story.
- "Game Ako Speaks!": this is the content for commentary and opinion piece. This will be a short-form content around 10-15 minutes and can be an article or video. The content will come from news, trends and recent topics in the video game industry.
- "Game Ako Chronicles Blog": this is a written content that will be published in the Game Ako Chronicles website. These are news, opinion pieces and game review.
- "NewsKoPo": this is a live gaming podcast every week sunday 7pm where we discuss news and trends in the video game industry. This is live in both Facebook and YouTube. We have a talking point list when we discuss things and I have co host on this.
