## 1.0 additions — late-game (spoiler-light: includes Deep North names)

| Tool | Crafted at | Materials | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Snow Shovel | Black Forge (lvl 3) | 5 Finewood, 4 Flametal, 2 Embers | Clears deep snow for building; deals no damage, trains Farming; quality 3, 200 durability |
| Grappling Hook | Black Forge | 10 Yggdrasil Wood, 10 Refined Eitr, 4 Mandible, 1 Hook | Traversal tool; 15 stamina per shot, 10 pierce, 300 durability |

New 1.0 axe lines also chop trees: Nord Axe / Nord Greataxe (cast at Black Forge lvl 4 from Bloodgold + mould + Timberwood), upgraded to Frostfire / Thunderblood versions; plus the early "Early Axes" workbench craft using found Curious/Mysterious Axe Heads.

## Not craftable (so not on the checklist)

- Fishing Rod — buy from Haldor for 350 coins
- Serving Tray — buy from the Bog Witch for 140 coins
- Dvergr Tankard — found in Mistlands structures (not crafted)
- Basic Fishing Bait — bought from Haldor; the 8 advanced baits are crafted at a Food Preparation Table (20 Bait + 1 trophy): Mossy (Troll), Sticky (Abomination), Stingy (Fuling), Heavy (Serpent), Misty (Lox), Hot (Warrior), Cold (Fenring), Frosty (Drake)
