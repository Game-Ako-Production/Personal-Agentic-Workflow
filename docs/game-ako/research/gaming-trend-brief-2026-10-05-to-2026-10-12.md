## A. Industry business & platform news (strongest commentary material)

### 1. Console hardware sales crater as prices climb
- **Finding:** US console hardware unit sales are at multi-year lows while console prices have risen. Research firm Circana's Mat Piscatella posted (Bluesky, Oct 11) that for 2026 year-to-date ending August, Xbox US hardware units were down 33% vs. a year ago (all-time low) and PlayStation units down 25% (lowest since 2013), attributing the drop partly to price sensitivity amid the memory/chip shortage.
- **Verification:** ✅ Verified. Backed by the Circana analyst's own posted figures and reported independently by Tom's Hardware (Oct 11) and Yahoo Finance (Oct 9, "13-year low"). Note: this is **US retail data through August**, not a single week; the "$800 Series X / $900 PS5 Pro" figures are current pricing, and the "13-year low" framing is the outlets' summary.
- **Evidence:** 33% Xbox unit decline; 25% PlayStation unit decline; Xbox at all-time US low; PlayStation lowest since 2013.
- **Game Ako relevance:** Directly ties to the PHP price angle Filipino buyers care about — "bakit ganito kamahal ang console ngayon?" is a strong commentary/livestream topic; can be paired with Sony's disc exit.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/console-gaming/console-market-craters-as-xbox-sales-crash-33-percent-to-the-lowest-since-launch-25-years-ago-ps5-sales-drop-25-percent-steep-price-hikes-push-series-x-to-usd800-and-ps5-pro-to-usd900
  - https://finance.yahoo.com/ (Yahoo Finance, Oct 9)
  - Piscatella Bluesky, Oct 11.

### 2. Another brutal layoff week
- **Finding:** Multiple studios cut staff this week: **Supermassive Games** (Until Dawn, Little Nightmares III) let go **more than 70** people (Eurogamer, Oct 5, per the studio's IT director); Xbox's **Ninja Theory** began layoffs after Xbox proposed its closure Sept 22 (GamesIndustry.biz, Oct 5); **Gravity Well** (Respawn alumni) lost funding and cut **40+** (GamesIndustry.biz, Oct 7); **Undead Labs**' boss commented on layoffs he was "reluctant to share" numbers on.
- **Verification:** ✅ Verified for Supermassive, Ninja Theory, Gravity Well, and Undead Labs (corroborated by Eurogamer, Insider Gaming, GamesIndustry.biz, Game Developer). **⚠️ Not fully verified — separate:** reported anxiety at **The Coalition** (Gears of War: E-Day) and a **Warhorse Studios** cost warning are *fears/warnings*, not confirmed layoffs (PC Gamer, Oct 5). Several content-farm sites falsely call these "confirmed cuts."
- **Game Ako relevance:** Sustained "gaming layoffs" narrative is prime podcast/commentary material; caution needed to avoid repeating the false "The Coalition layoffs confirmed" claim.

### 9. Newzoo: F2P flat, but gacha RPGs drive PC growth
- **Finding:** Newzoo's report says free-to-play isn't dying: F2P's share of playtime fell ~2.9 points (2021→2025) to **29.3%** but absolute hours stayed broadly flat; on **PC, F2P playtime grew**, with gacha RPGs cited as a driver. Annual F2P live-service launches on PC/console fell ~31% since the early 2020s.
- **Verification:** ✅ Verified as a **source-attributed** finding (GamesIndustry.biz, Oct 5, reporting Newzoo's Game Performance Monitor; methodology **excludes China, India, and mobile**).
- **Game Ako relevance:** Data-backed counter to "F2P is dead"; useful for commentary on where gacha fits.
- **Source:** https://www.gamesindustry.biz/free-to-play-playtime-is-broadly-flat-as-gacha-rpgs-drive-pc-growth-says-newzoo

## C. Policy / regulation

### 13. EU Kids Act worries game industry groups
- **Finding:** The proposed **EU Kids Act** (child online-safety regulation) has drawn warnings that age-assurance and safety-by-design duties could hurt small creators and old/re-released games (EUobserver, Oct 9). The proposal itself was tabled earlier (Sept 2026).
- **Verification:** ✅ Verified — the **proposal** is documented on EUR-Lex; the **industry criticism** is an attributed position from trade groups, not an established outcome.
- **Game Ako relevance:** Court the global/preservation-policy conversation; "regulation vs. games" is evergreen commentary.
- **Sources:**
