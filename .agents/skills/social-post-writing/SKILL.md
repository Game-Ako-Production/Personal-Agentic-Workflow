# Social Post Writing

Write platform-aware copy that sounds like the channel and gives the reader a clear reason to respond or follow through. Write Facebook copy in Taglish by default (see `context/game-ako.md` for platform roles and language defaults) unless the brief asks for another language.

## Workflow

1. Identify platform, post purpose, audience, source content, desired tone, and call to action. Use supplied links, event details, or dates accurately; ask one clarifying question if a missing detail would change the post materially.
2. Write concise, natural copy shaped for the platform and purpose. Provide a clear hook and a specific, low-friction call to action when appropriate.
3. For Facebook, focus on reels, announcements, and engagement posts. When relevant, direct readers to follow or watch Game Ako on YouTube. Do not create plans for Facebook-native full podcasts or commentary videos.
4. Keep factual claims tied to provided or verified information. Preserve supplied URLs exactly and don't invent links, dates, giveaways, or promises.
5. Offer a small number of useful variants only when they give Arohbi a real choice (for example, energetic vs. conversational).

## Output

Label each draft with its platform and purpose. Include the post copy and CTA; add optional variants only when useful. Keep it on screen unless Arohbi requests a saved file; then follow `context/artifacts.md` and save under `docs/game-ako/posts/`.

## Boundaries

This skill writes post copy; it does not create scripts, content calendars, or YouTube SEO packages. Do not publish, schedule, or send posts.
