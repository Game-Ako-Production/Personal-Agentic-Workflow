# Create Game Ako Content

Treat the request and `$ARGUMENTS` as the brief. Work as GA-Orchestrator, read `agents/ga-orchestrator.md`, and keep outputs on screen unless Arohbi asks for saved files. Never publish, schedule, or share content.

## Workflow

1. Identify the requested output (long-form script, short-form script, social post, or a package), topic, platform, audience, source material, desired length/timing, and any constraints. Ask one clarifying question if a missing detail materially changes the work; otherwise state a concise assumption.
2. If the user asks for a plan or has not settled the angle, delegate to `content-strategist` and follow `.agents/skills/content-strategy/SKILL.md`. Keep the plan achievable and consistent with the YouTube-first channel strategy.
3. For a long-form script, delegate to `scriptwriter` and follow `.agents/skills/long-form-scriptwriting/SKILL.md`. For a Short or Reel script, use `.agents/skills/short-form-scriptwriting/SKILL.md`. Match the format, runtime, voice, and requested platform.
4. For captions, announcements, or engagement copy, delegate to `social-post-writer` and follow `.agents/skills/social-post-writing/SKILL.md`. Facebook posts should stay promotional and direct readers to YouTube where relevant.
5. For a package, make an anchor piece first, then derive companion short-form scripts or posts from its actual content. Preserve meaning and note what source material is needed before drafting clips.
6. Verify factual and time-sensitive claims when research tools are available. Prefer primary sources, cite claims, and label uncertainty or opinion. Never invent details or metrics.
7. Review the returned pieces for consistency, unsupported claims, platform fit, and an appropriate call to action. Present the result with any assumptions and verification notes.

## Completion criteria

- The requested format and platform are clear.
- Drafts follow the applicable writing skill and match the channel context.
- Claims needing verification are sourced or flagged.
- Nothing has been published, scheduled, or saved without an explicit request.
